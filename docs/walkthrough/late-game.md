# Late game: the road to Meduna

By now you should hold the mining towns and a serious income — if not, go back to the
[mid game](mid-game.md). What remains is the southern quarter of the map: two hidden
facilities (Tixa and Orta), a junkyard that becomes your forward base (Estoni), the
Queen's favorite town (Balime), and finally Meduna itself.

!!! warning "Full spoilers ahead"
    This page spoils every late-game location, the palace layout, and the ending of
    Jagged Alliance 2. See [how to use the walkthrough](index.md) if you want a
    spoiler-lighter route overview first.

## The late-game map

| Location | Sector(s) | What's there |
| -------- | --------- | ------------ |
| Estoni | I6 | Junkyard, defunct gas station, Jake's shop, helicopter refueling (after a quest) |
| Tixa | J9 (+ dungeon below) | Secret prison; Dynamo and Shank are held here |
| Orta | K4 (+ facility below) | Hidden Rocket Rifle manufacturing plant |
| Balime | L11, L12 | Wealthy loyalist town, museum, three shops |
| Meduna | N3, N4, N5, O3, O4, P3 (+ underground) | The capital: airport, SAM site, arena, palace |

Tixa and Orta start hidden — they do not appear on your map until you learn about them
(see below). Neither can hold militia, and the Queen counterattacks both persistently
after you take them, so garrison them with mercs or mobile militia if you want to keep
them.

## Late-game enemies in 1.13

1.13 scales equipment with **game progress**: as your campaign advances, items of
higher "coolness" appear in shops and in enemy hands. By the time you approach Meduna
you will be facing troops with top-tier gear — around the capital that includes tanks,
and defenders armed with mortars, RPGs, and heavy machine guns. Two settings control
how this feels:

- **Progress Speed of Item Choices** (New Game screen) sets how fast better equipment
  becomes available to both you and the enemy. On *Very Slow* you fight the pistol/SMG
  war much longer; on *Fast* the late game arrives early.
- **`DROP_ALL`** (in `JA2_Options.ini`; on the New Game screen in r7609) makes enemies
  drop all their items instead of a random selection — which in the late game means
  mountains of high-end loot after every battle.

Difficulty also matters more now: higher difficulty levels raise the Queen's total army
size, the share of elite troops in patrols, and give enemies extra APs. See
[New Game options](../playing/new-game-options.md) and
[recommended settings](../configuration/recommended-settings.md) for details, and the
[glossary](../reference/glossary.md) for the progress/coolness jargon.

Meduna itself has a 1.13-specific twist: several of its sectors trigger **enemy
reinforcements from adjacent, unconquered sectors right after you capture them**. Each
sector section below notes where they come from.

## Estoni (I6): the junkyard

Estoni is a single-sector town: a junkyard, a defunct gas station, a shack, and three
inhabitants. Militia cannot be trained here, and strategically the enemy treats it like
any other road sector — but it is one of the most useful bases in the late game.

- **Jake Cameron** runs the junkyard shop, 24 hours a day (he doesn't mind being woken
  up). He is a reliable source of glass jars, medical kits and first aid kits (usually
  used — combine them into full ones), canteens and break lights, and occasionally
  stocks tool kits, ceramic plates, and Compound 18. He pays better than anyone else
  for junk items (silver platters, watches, booze, and the like) and carries a small
  selection of pistols, revolvers, and knives.
- **Skipper**, an old homeless man rummaging through the junkyard, is quietly one of
  the most important NPCs in the late game — see the Orta section below.
- **Kevin "Maddog" Cameron**, Jake's son, is recruitable — see
  [recruitable NPCs](npcs-recruitment.md).

Talk to Jake with a merc who has decent Leadership and he asks you to check up on
**Breeham "Shank" Druz**, a kid from Balime who was thrown into the prison at Tixa.
This starts the *Rescue Shank* quest. Once you free Shank (see Tixa below) and bring
him back to Estoni to talk to Jake, Jake starts selling fuel from his black-market
stash — Estoni then works as a refueling station and landing pad for Skyrider's
helicopter.

!!! tip "1.13: the Junkyard facility"
    In 1.13, Estoni's junkyard is a **facility** that increases the efficiency of item,
    vehicle, and helicopter repair. Combined with the fuel supply, that makes Estoni an
    ideal mid-to-late-game base camp — park mobile militia in the sector to defend it,
    since you cannot train any there. A garrison here also slows down and gives early
    warning of enemy patrols heading for your towns.

!!! note "Shank not showing up as a chopper pad?"
    A known bug can prevent Skyrider from recognizing the new refueling site after
    Shank talks to Jake. Move a merc out of Estoni and back in (keeping at least one
    merc in the sector) to trigger the speech.

## Tixa (J9): the secret prison

Tixa is a hidden prison south of Cambria. Its location is revealed by talking to
**Matt Duncan**, the head miner in Alma — he asks you to break his brother **Greg
"Dynamo" Duncan** out of it (the *Free Dynamo From Tixa* quest). Beneath the prison is
a dungeon where political prisoners are tortured and held for execution.

The complex is a single large L-shaped building: cells in the western half, staff
quarters, barracks and offices in the east, the canteen in the center. Solitary
confinement cells stand isolated west of the main building, and a small house on the
grounds east of it belongs to the warden, **Christine "Warden" Woltz**. A fence rings
the whole complex — without wire cutters, the only way in is the gate on the south
side.

### The alarm and how to beat it

When you are spotted or open fire, the Warden sounds the alarm and floods the prison
with tear gas. The guards put on their gas masks when the alarm sounds, then fight you
in the gas; the prisoners in their cells are unaffected. There is a better way, and the
Warden's husband will hand it to you:

1. Approach from the **east**, preferably at night, and stealth into the house at the
   southeast corner.
2. Be nice to **Brewster Woltz**, the Warden's husband. He tells you everything about
   his wife, and if you keep talking to him he gives you the key to her office.
3. At **17:00** the Warden leaves her office — sneak into the jail house then to catch
   her off guard. (It takes about 3 hours to walk from the adjacent sector J10, so
   leave J10 around 14:00.) Outside that window she is in the office.
4. Kill her **before she reaches the switches**, ideally with suppressed weapons.

Her office has two switches: a red button that alerts the guards to mask up, and a
flip-switch that floods the prison with tear gas. If you kill the Warden quietly, you
can flip the gas switch yourself **without** the mask warning — the guards inside get
gassed with no chance to react. Bring your own gas masks, move fast, and you can strip
unconscious guards of their (often good) weapons. Enemies outside still patrol
regularly, so stay low and use the gas clouds as concealment.

### Dynamo

Dynamo is locked in a cell on the western side of the ground floor. He is wounded —
give him first aid, then talk to him again to complete Matt Duncan's quest (this also
boosts loyalty in Alma). A merc with modest Leadership can then recruit him for
$50/day — or **for free** if he refuses your first offer and you ask again.

### The dungeon and Shank

The stairs down are behind a locked door in the **northeast corner** of the building —
pick the lock. Below, enemies hold the long hall near the entrance and can interrupt
careless mercs; use the table as cover and watch the door directly facing the stairs,
which enemies sometimes open. Clear the main hallway, the medical area, and the torture
chambers; a lone enemy with a CAWS lurks near the torture chamber and the last defender
usually waits in the guards' leisure room at the end of the hallway.

**Breeham "Shank" Druz** is locked in a cell down here. Break him out and talk to him:
he tells his story, offers to join you for $20/day, and offers to secure a fuel source
if he can get to Estoni — which completes Jake's *Rescue Shank* quest and unlocks the
helicopter refueling described above. Both recruits are covered in more detail on the
[recruitable NPCs page](npcs-recruitment.md).

### Loot

- One of the guards always drops a **Steyr AUG**.
- Lockers in the guards' quarters near the Warden's office hold **Spectra vests,
  leggings, and helmets**.
- Most defenders drop **gas masks**, and more sit in lockers all over the complex.

!!! note "Sci-Fi mode"
    In Sci-Fi mode there is a **Crepitus lair** further underground beneath the
    dungeon. Wiping it out immediately triggers a Crepitus attack on your mine; if you
    raid it after killing the Crepitus Queen, the lair is empty.

## Orta (K4): the secret weapons facility

Orta never appears on your map at the start — it is always at sector K4, and you can
learn its location from **General Theo Humphey** in Alma. From the outside it poses as
a Bloodcat research facility, but it is suspiciously well guarded: it is actually a
hidden manufacturing plant for **Rocket Rifles**.

The door to the underground facility is heavily locked, set in an indestructible wall,
and nearly impossible to pick. You have three ways in:

1. **Skipper's keycard** — give Skipper in Estoni *any* item (value doesn't matter) and
   he hands you a keycard for the Orta basement.
2. **Bribe Walter Bazzon** — this scientist on the ground floor of the main building
   opens the passage for $20,000. Killing him costs no loyalty, and if you kill him
   after paying he drops your money (plus his own $50).
3. **Blow the wall** — the door itself is indestructible, but firing a LAW at the wall
   just south of the door collapses it (explosives may also work).

The underground levels are defended even more heavily than the surface. One switch in
the main hallway opens all the doors. Once the facility is secure, find
**Dr. Ernest Poppin** in the back room and keep threatening him — he eventually gives
in and opens a store room **full of Rocket Rifles**.

!!! tip "Do Estoni first"
    An item worth a dollar handed to Skipper saves you $20,000 or a LAW rocket. Stop by
    the junkyard before you assault Orta.

Like Tixa, Orta counts as strategically important to the Queen: expect a serious
retake attempt shortly after you capture it, and repeated attempts afterwards. You
cannot train or station trained militia here, so keep mercs or mobile militia around.

## Balime (L11–L12): the Queen's favorites

Balime, on the southern coast, is where Arulco's rich live — "the chosen friends of
Queen Deidranna". Most of the wealthy inhabitants flee to Meduna as you approach,
leaving the manors largely derelict. There is no mine here, but the town matters for
its shops, its museum, and as your doorstep to the capital.

### Taking the town

- **L11 (Balime West).** Lots of open space between houses, so house-to-house fighting
  runs at longer ranges than in other towns, and there are almost no accessible flat
  roofs — all vantage points are on the ground. Several soldiers bunker up in the manor
  house at the southern end; its doors are trapped, and lethal interrupts are easy to
  walk into even at night.
- **L12 (Balime East).** The main avenue is a sniper corridor for both sides, and most
  defenders fight along it. Night ops are unusually hard here because the streets and
  stores are dotted with lights. The defenders carry assault and sniper rifles, so
  engage at range or sneak. The museum holds nobody but Eldin — you can bypass it
  during the battle, though watch for enemies hiding in its entry hallway.

### Loyalty and militia

Balime is heavily aligned with Deidranna's regime. When you conquer it, loyalty starts
**below 20%**, which makes [militia training](../playing/features/militia.md)
impossible at first. Completing the *Chalice of Chance* quest for Chitzena is the
practical way to push loyalty over the 20% threshold so you can start training.

### The museum and the Chalice of Chance

The museum in L12 houses the diamond-encrusted Golden Chalice of Chance in a display
case at the back, guarded around the clock by **Eldin Fiddes**, an elderly WWII veteran
who doubles as tour guide and night watchman. The display case has a siren tripwire; a
switch in the security office (or a merc with very high Mechanical skill and the
Electronics trait) disables it. Do **not** simply shoot Eldin — killing him cripples
your already-poor loyalty in Balime. Kingpin in San Mona pays $20,000 for the Chalice;
returning it to Yanni in Chitzena instead grants instant 100% loyalty there plus a 6%
loyalty boost in all your city sectors. The full quest, including how to get it from
Kingpin and stealth options, is on the [side quests page](side-quests.md).

### Shops

| Shopkeeper | Sector | Deals in |
| ---------- | ------ | -------- |
| Franz Hinkle | L12 | Electronics shop; buys most firearms and armor, open to barter |
| Sam Rozen | L12 | Tool store; sells only, buys nothing |
| Howard Filmore | L12 | Pharmacy; sells medical gear, buys nothing |
| Dave Gerard | L10 (west of town) | Gas station |

## Meduna: the capital

Meduna sits in the southwest corner of the map — six surface sectors (N3, N4, N5, O3,
O4, P3) plus an underground bunker, containing the airport, the last SAM site, a
bloodcat arena, the gardens, a residential district, and the palace.

Before you even reach the city: the surrounding sectors **M3, M4, M5, and N6 all
contain heavy resistance and tanks**. Treat clearing them as part of the assault plan,
and bring anti-tank weapons and plenty of ammo for everything below.

### N3 — the airport (3 tanks)

Tanks dot the landscape — one waits very close to the southeast corner, a nasty
surprise if you enter near there. Sandbags block most roads, and machine-gun nests ring
the airport proper. The cleanest approach is from the **northeast**, which keeps you
away from the armor and lets you pull soldiers out of their entrenchments. If you trust
your tank-busting, entering from the southwest and killing the nearest tank immediately
lets you occupy the army's own sandbag lines. Several soldiers linger inside the
warehouse and rec room even after the main fight. *1.13:* enemy reinforcements arrive
in force from the south after you capture the sector, unless you already hold it.

### N4 — the SAM site (2 tanks)

The most heavily defended SAM site in Arulco: heavy armor, a thick line of sandbag
nests, and many defenders with mortars, RPGs, and heavy machine guns. Bring multiple
squads and expect a grueling, ammo-hungry fight. The enemy considers the sector
strategically vital and spends days trying to retake it if you grab it early. *1.13:*
reinforcements come in force from the south after capture, unless that sector is
already yours.

### N5 — the bloodcat arena

Deidranna's arena, where dissenters are fed to bloodcats, fills most of the sector. All
the soldiers are inside the building; the entrance is on the southeast side and the
structure has almost no windows, so it stays quiet until you round the front. At the
entrance, expect heavy resistance plus at least half a dozen **tamed bloodcats** that
rush your mercs (they never attack the army) — pack hollow-point ammo. The few windows
on the southwest end will be used to shoot at your flank.

!!! danger "The walls are booby-trapped"
    A buried layer of stun grenades completely surrounds each of the four corner
    towers, and that layer is itself ringed by a layer of mines. Tread carefully.

*1.13:* possible reinforcements from the east/southeast — a handful of heavily armored
soldiers, some carrying break lights and grenades (including mustard gas), and at least
one with a LAW he is not afraid to use.

### O3 — the gardens and the secret tunnel (2 tanks)

A maze of hedges and statuary along a single coastal road. Hedges give cover to both
sides, and bullets penetrate a single hedge layer — exploit that when a merc gets
caught at a corner. The two tanks only cover two short stretches of road on the east
side and can be snuck around. The western side is clear of enemies but **studded with
mines** — metal detectors are mandatory there. *1.13:* reinforcements arrive in force
from the south after capture; you can re-lay the mines you lift from the beach along
the southern border to funnel the counterattack, and there are enough mines to disarm
to train a merc's Explosives skill substantially — handy for the bunker below.

???+ note "The secret palace entrance (spoiler)"
    Somewhere in the buildings of this area is a **remote control** (you may need to
    repair it first). Bring it to the destroyed statue between the two still-standing
    statues in the middle of the garden maze and *use* it: a secret entrance opens to
    an underground tunnel (O3-1) leading to the northern section of the palace's secret
    bunker. Entering this way is the only way you can normally obtain an **Automatic
    Rocket Rifle**. Opening the tunnel can also trigger a massive assault on O3, so be
    ready. The tunnel itself is straightforward with little resistance (sometimes, due
    to a glitch, the enemies inside don't spawn at all).

### O4 — the residential district (1 tank)

Fighting centers on sandbag emplacements at the street intersections; as always in
dense urban combat, watch windows and doorways. Despite all the homes and stores, there
are no NPCs or shopkeepers here. *1.13:* reinforcements come from the west after
capture if that side isn't yours yet — though if the army has already spent its
reserves reinforcing your earlier conquests, resistance here can turn out surprisingly
thin.

### P3 — the palace

For a frontal assault, bring a **LAW or explosives: the front gate is sealed** and
otherwise impassable. Cover the breach and let the defenders come to you, then move in
behind smoke grenades to avoid interrupts. Many doors are trapped — bring a merc with
good Mechanical skill, or blow the doors open. A group of enemies holds the throne room
no matter what chaos erupts outside.

Cleared the throne room and no Queen? Search the rooms (mind the trapped doors) for a
**trapdoor in the fireplace of the piano room** — it leads down into the secret bunker.

### P3-1 — the secret bunker

You reach the bunker either from the palace fireplace or covertly through the O3 garden
tunnel. Expect a large number of steel doors (a merc with high Mechanical skill opens
them quietly) and explosive traps (high Explosives skill spots them in advance).

## The Queen, Elliot, and Joe

Three named characters wait at the end of the road:

- **Elliot**, the Queen's battered advisor — slapped on camera after every major battle
  you win, and finally shot in the head (he survives, and apologizes for it) when he
  reports your approach to Meduna. You find him in a section of the palace. He will not
  fight you or hinder you, cannot be bribed, bought, or recruited, and refuses to
  reveal where the Queen is.
- **Joseph "Joe" Papanus**, Deidranna's enforcer/bodyguard: near-maximum combat stats,
  an H&K 21 with 7.62 NATO magazines, Spectra helmet and vest, and the Auto Weapons and
  Martial Arts skills.
- **Queen Deidranna** herself, either in the throne room (guarded by elites) or down in
  the secret shelter. She fights with an Auto Rocket Rifle loaded with HEAP
  mini-rockets and wears a full Compound 18 Spectra set (helmet, vest, leggings) — she
  has the Auto Weapons skill, the Psycho trait, and 90 marksmanship, so do not treat
  her as a pushover.

!!! tip "Buying Joe off"
    Joe can be bribed — but you must act within the first tactical round after spotting
    him. Run a merc up to him and hand over a large sum of cash; if you pull it off, he
    walks away ("She is all yours. I'm out of here.") and leaves you to the Queen.

### Victory

Killing Deidranna is the **only** main objective of the game: the campaign ends the
moment she dies, and you do not need to clear the throne room or the rest of Meduna
first. Every playable merc has a unique line for delivering the deathblow, so choose
your executioner with care.

Two curiosities from the endgame, for completeness:

- If any of your mercs are captured or surrender, an interrogation scene plays at the
  **N7 military base**, where Deidranna appears in person alongside Elliot and Joe (Joe
  does the punching). A well-known glitch lets players end the game right there by
  planting a mine in the doorway she walks through before the scene.
- Deidranna murdered her husband Enrico Chivaldori's father, framed Enrico, and seized
  power — which is why Enrico hired you in the first place. Killing her completes the
  contract that started on day one.

Congratulations, Commander — Arulco is free.
